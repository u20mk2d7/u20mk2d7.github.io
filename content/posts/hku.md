# HKU application plan

Goal: study at the University of Hong Kong (HKU) in a programme that leads toward quant development: computer science, statistics, data science, or finance.

## My qualifications

- Vietnamese high school diploma (THPT)
- Vietnamese college diploma (cao đẳng) in IT

## Possible routes

Ordered from most likely to least likely. Each one needs confirmation from HKU admissions.

1. **Year 1 entry as an international applicant.** Apply with high school results and IELTS. To confirm: are Vietnamese THPT results accepted, and with what minimum? Can any cao đẳng credits transfer?
2. **Hong Kong AD/HD, then HKU senior year.** Study a 2-year Associate Degree or Higher Diploma at a Hong Kong community college, then apply for a senior-year place. To confirm: are non-local students on a Hong Kong AD/HD eligible? Places are few.
3. **HKU SPACE top-up degree.** Shorter, and a cao đẳng in IT may qualify. But the degree is awarded by the partner university, not by HKU.

## Requirements found so far

| Item | Requirement | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IELTS, undergraduate | 6.5 overall, at least 6.0 in Writing and Speaking, one attempt, taken within 2 years before 1 September of entry | [HKU English requirement](https://admissions.hku.hk/apply/international-qualifications/english-language-requirement) |
| IELTS, BEcon&Fin | 7.0 overall, 6.5 in each section | [HKU FBE FAQ](https://www4.fbe.hku.hk/ug/admission/faq/non-jupas-applicants) |
| Senior-year places | Only for final-year students or graduates of a full-time AD/HD (2+ years) from a Hong Kong community college | [HKU admissions](https://admissions.hku.hk/node/146) |
| Quantitative programmes | Very competitive (e.g. IB 38–39 or A-level 2A* 1A for Statistical Decision Sciences, Computing and Data Science) | [HKU International Admissions 2026](https://admissions.hku.hk/sites/default/files/2025-10/HKU-International-Admissions-Information-2026.pdf) |

**My IELTS target:** 7.0 overall, no section below 6.5. This covers every requirement above.

## Questions to send HKU admissions

- [ ] Can credits from my cao đẳng in IT be transferred?
- [ ] Are non-local students on a Hong Kong AD/HD eligible for senior-year places?

## Timeline

1. **Oct 2026:** send the questions to HKU admissions. Choose a route from their answer.
2. **Oct 2026 – 2027:** English Stage 1 and 2 (see the study plan). Mock IELTS every 6–8 weeks once in Stage 2.
3. **When mock tests are steady at 7.0:** book the official IELTS Academic. Remember it must be within 2 years of entry.
4. **Application cycle:** the first one after the IELTS result, most likely for 2029 entry. Earlier if English moves faster.
