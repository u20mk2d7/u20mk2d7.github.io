## HKU plan

My qualifications: Vietnamese high school diploma (THPT) and a Vietnamese college diploma

**Routes, best first:**

1. **Year 1 entry as an international applicant.** Apply with high school results plus IELTS. A cao đẳng is an overseas sub-degree, so it does not qualify for HKU's senior-year places; ask HKU whether any of its credits can transfer.
2. **2-year Associate Degree / Higher Diploma at a Hong Kong community college, then HKU senior year.** Only if HKU confirms that non-local students on a Hong Kong AD/HD are eligible. Places are few: 34 senior-year places across seven Engineering programmes.
3. **HKU SPACE top-up degree.** Shorter, but the degree is awarded by the partner university, not HKU.

**Targets:**

- [ ] IELTS Academic: HKU minimum is 6.5 overall with at least 6.0 in Writing and Speaking, in one attempt, taken within 2 years before 1 September of entry. Some programmes ask more (BEcon&Fin: 7.0, 6.5 in each section). My target: 7.0 with no section below 6.5.
- [ ] Academic results: quantitative programmes (Statistical Decision Sciences, Actuarial Science, Computing and Data Science) are very competitive. Check whether HKU accepts Vietnamese THPT results directly, or whether SAT/AP would strengthen the application.

**Questions to send HKU admissions:**

- [ ] Does HKU accept Vietnamese THPT results for Year 1 entry, and what minimum average?
- [ ] Can credits from a Vietnamese cao đẳng be transferred?
- [ ] Are non-local students on a Hong Kong AD/HD eligible for senior-year places?
- [ ] This year's application dates for international applicants.

**Timing:** apply in the first cycle after a mock IELTS test is steady at the target. From band 3, that is most likely the cycle for 2029 entry. Re-check after every mock test.
