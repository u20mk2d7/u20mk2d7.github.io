Sequential curriculum (one topic at a time)

Work strictly top to bottom within each track. Do not jump to advanced topics until the current checkbox is verified with code, benchmarks, or solved problems.

Track 1 — 08:00 weekdays: blustone & solo trading engine

  1. Zero-allocation core primitives: fixed_point, Micros, OrderType::LimitMakerVerify zero dynamic allocations on hot path; clean under ASan + UBSan.
  2. Lock-free SPSC ring buffer + CPU core pinningPin ingress and strategy threads to isolated Ryzen cores (pthread_setaffinity_np); verify cache-line padding.
  3. Cache-friendly L2 Order Book & Binance SBE binary decoderReplace pointer-heavy trees with flat arrays/open-addressing maps; zero-copy SBE parsing.
  4. Network RTT benchmark & Tokyo VPS co-location checkMeasure wire-to-wire p50/p99 latency to Binance Spot/Futures; deploy Tokyo VPS if p50 > 20 ms.
  5. Phase 3: Live market data capture & binary packet loggerRun continuous tick-by-tick capture to disk without stalling the ingress thread.
  6. Phase 4: Deterministic replay engine & markout simulatorReplay captured SBE streams with realistic queue-position and maker/taker fee modeling.
  7. Live paper-to-micro-capital execution & hard kill-switchImplement max drawdown, rate-limit backoff, and stale-book circuit breakers before live capital.

Track 2 — Tue & Thu 21:00: Modern C++20/26 & hardware sympathy

  1. C++17/20/26 Concurrency & Memory ModelFinish Packt Concurrency + C++ Concurrency in Action (Williams): atomics, acquire/release, memory barriers.
  2. Compile-time polymorphism & zero-overhead abstractionsReplace virtual dispatch with templates, concepts, CRTP, std::span, std::bit_cast, and [[likely]].
  3. Memory layout, custom arena/pool allocators & false sharingUse alignas(64), jemalloc, and std::pmr; inspect struct padding with pahole.
  4. CPU profiling & assembly inspection on Debian 13Master perf stat -d, perf record flame graphs, valgrind --tool=cachegrind, and Compiler Explorer assembly.
  5. SIMD vectorization (AVX2 intrinsics)Vectorize order book level updates, rolling statistical windows, and binary parsing.

Track 3 — Mon 21:00: Linux kernel & low-latency networking

  1. Linux OS noise reduction & deterministic schedulingConfigure isolcpus, nohz_full, SCHED_FIFO real-time priority, and disable CPU frequency scaling.
  2. Virtual memory, page faults & HugePagesLock memory with mlockall(MCL_CURRENT | MCL_FUTURE) and pre-fault 2MB HugePages to eliminate minor page faults.
  3. Low-latency BSD sockets & epoll busy-pollingTune TCP_NODELAY, SO_BUSY_POLL, socket ring buffers (rmem_max), and non-blocking event loops.
  4. Asynchronous I/O with io_uringZero-syscall disk logging for market capture and asynchronous network submission.
  5. Kernel-bypass architectures (AF_XDP, DPDK, Solarflare ef_vi)Study user-space packet processing and NIC ring buffers for institutional HFT interviews.

Track 4 — 09:00 weekdays: Mathematics & statistical validation

  1. Algebra: Equations & Inequalities & Functionsfast-trackSelf-test first; clear quickly to unlock calculus.
  2. Single-variable Calculus (limits, derivatives, integrals, Taylor series)Khan Academy AP Calculus AB/BC or MIT OCW 18.01.
  3. Linear Algebra for MLMatrices, eigenvalues, covariance matrices, OLS regression, and PCA.
  4. Probability Foundations & Statistical EstimationExpectation, variance, conditional probability, and confidence intervals for markout edge validation.
  5. Markov Chains & Monte Carlo + OpenIntro StatisticsQueue-position state transitions, fill simulation, and hypothesis testing (t-tests, p-values).
  6. Python research stack (pandas, polars, numpy, matplotlib)Analyze C++-captured Parquet/binary logs and plot markout decay curves.

Track 5 — Wed 21:00 & Sat 09:00: Microstructure & solo trading edge

  1. Market mechanics & Limit Order Book dynamicsRead Trading and Exchanges (Harris): order types, matching priority, maker-taker fees, and rebates.
  2. Short-horizon alpha signals: OBI, Micro-Price & OFIImplement Order Book Imbalance, volume-weighted Micro-Price, and Order Flow Imbalance on captured ticks.
  3. Adverse selection & toxic flow detectionMeasure markout at 10ms, 100ms, 1s, and 5s horizons; cancel stale quotes when cross-exchange lead moves.
  4. Market-making inventory control (Avellaneda-Stoikov)Read Algorithmic and High-Frequency Trading (Cartea et al.); skew bid/ask quotes based on inventory risk.
  5. Backtest overfitting & execution hygieneStudy Bailey et al. ("Probability of Backtest Overfitting"); account for latency jitter and partial fills.

Track 6 — Fri 21:00 & Sat 21:00: NEU Finance, Algos & Interviews
